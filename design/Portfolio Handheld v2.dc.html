YASHPUNIA.XYZ — HANDHELD v2 · TURN 4

The console stays.
The screen grows up.

This builds on 2a and 1a. The hardware is quieter and more physical, and the screen now runs a proper console home: key art first, one title, four facts, then a shelf of games.

Art does the talking
Full-bleed key art replaces boxed thumbnails. The UI just labels it.
Fewer, calmer labels
Monospace caps only for tiny counters. Everything else is plain sentence case.
Green means "selected"
No glowing buttons. The accent marks focus, plus a single power LED.
Hardware you'd buy
A matte shell, a recessed screen, legends printed under the buttons, and no fake decoration.
Buttons are shortcuts
Everything on screen is clickable or tappable. The controls just speed it up.
Fat-finger safe
Mobile targets are at least 44px, with primary actions at 48px+ in the thumb zone.
